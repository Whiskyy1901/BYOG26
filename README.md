# BLAZE

**You can't escape the fire, but you can sure get a high score before it gets you.**

Blaze is a top-down survival shooter where nothing lasts. You're standing on a sheet of paper, and it's on fire.

Every gun you pick up burns out on a timer, and then you're back to the pistol. You never actually get stronger, you just get short windows of power, so use them while you have them.

Made in Unity for the **BYOG game jam**. Theme: **Everything is Temporary**.

## About

The paper keeps burning in from the edges and your arena keeps shrinking. Clearing a wave pushes the fire back for a while, but you can't put it out. Eventually the whole sheet is ash, and so are you.

So what's the point? The score. You can't beat the fire, but you can decide what you do before it gets you: how many enemies you took down, how many waves you cleared, and what you did with the few seconds of power you were given.

## How It Works

- Kill enemies, get better weapons, and lose them when the timer runs out.
- Standing in the fire hurts.
- Clear a wave to push the fire back. It always comes back.
- Survive as long as you can. The run ends when the paper does.

## Enemies

| Enemy | Behavior |
|-------|----------|
| **Grunts** | Walk up, stop, then swing. Dodge the windup. |
| **Shooters** | Stay back and shoot you. Later ones have machine guns and snipers. |
| **Chargers** | Wind up, then dash at you. Later ones chain dashes. |
